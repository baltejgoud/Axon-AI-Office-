What Axon is

Most AI lives in a chat box. Axon gives yours an office: a team of coworkers who work side by side in your project, on the models you choose, with the tools you connect, and ask before anything is saved.

03 · Approval

Nothing saves
without your OK.

Reading your project is automatic. Writing to it is not. Every change arrives as a diff, and waits for you.

  • Writes, commands, commits and sub-agents each ask first. Running commands stays off until you turn it on.
  • Undo any saved change. Axon keeps the version each write replaced, and warns you if the file changed since.
  • An activity log of every tool call, with what was decided and by whom. Never with file contents.
  • Restore points snapshot all your saved data. Restoring backs up the current state first.

04 · Models

Any model.
Your keys.

Use Claude where it matters, and cheaper models like DeepSeek, Qwen or Kimi for the everyday work. Or run models free on your own machine with Ollama.

  1. Pick a service. OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter, Ollama, or any compatible endpoint.
  2. Paste your key. Axon checks it and picks the models. The key goes into your system’s key store.
  3. Choose per conversation. Each coworker’s conversation can run on a different model.
